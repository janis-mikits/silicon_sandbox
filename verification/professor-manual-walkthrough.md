The current Mac build has passed automated startup and persistence smoke checks. A person still needs to perform the steps below in the visible player. The default hotbar is **1 Source, 2 Wire, 3 AND, 4 SR, 5 Clock Link**; a published module normally enters the next free hotbar slot. Right-click a source *body* to change its current On/Off state; target its small free pin to attach a wire. Aim within 15 cells. `Esc` opens the paused control screen; `P` starts/stops the world clock; `I` opens Inspect on a pin, connector, component, or module body. `Esc` closes Inspect.
