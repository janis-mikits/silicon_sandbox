# First-playable professor walkthrough — manual acceptance sheet

This is the player-facing check for the five steps in [delivery and acceptance](../docs/delivery-and-acceptance.md#first-playable-professor-milestone). It has **not yet been performed**. Automated evidence is mapped in the [coverage audit](first-playable-coverage-audit.md), but that evidence cannot establish mouse targeting, panel readability, or whether a new player can complete the flow. Record the built-player version, date, machine, and any failure before calling the manual demonstration passed. Use a fresh world so earlier saved designs do not confuse the result.

Build first with `scripts/verify-unity.sh --editor '/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.3.24f1/Unity.app/Contents/MacOS/Unity' --target StandaloneOSX`, then open `UnityProject/Builds/macOS/SiliconSandbox.app`. The current Mac build has passed automated startup and persistence smoke checks. A person still needs to perform the steps below in the visible player. The default hotbar is **1 Source, 2 Wire, 3 AND, 4 SR, 5 Clock Link**; a published module normally enters the next free hotbar slot. Right-click a source *body* to change its current On/Off state; target its small free pin to attach a wire. Aim within 15 cells. `Esc` opens the paused control screen; `P` starts/stops the world clock; `I` opens Inspect on a pin, connector, component, or module body. `Esc` closes Inspect.

| Step | Player actions | Required observation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 — build | In the flat world, place two Sources and one AND with space around them. With Wire selected, right-click the first source's OUT pin and AND A pin; repeat from the second source OUT to AND B. Add a visible short connector from AND Y to a free floor cell so Y can be targeted. | The pins and all three one-bit connector routes are visible and selectable. The AND responds with the clock stopped. | Unrun |
| D2 — four states | Start with A Off and B On at value 1. Toggle A On. Configure B's On value to Z with `C` while aiming at its body; toggle A Off, then On. Configure B's On value to X. For each settled condition, inspect Y. Later, while the clock runs on the stateful circuit, fly until both instances are outside the camera view and return after another rising edge. | The AND sequence is `0&1→0`, `1&1→1`, `0&Z→0`, `1&Z→X`, `1&X→X`. Offscreen clock activity changes instance A from Q=1 to Q=0 while B holds Q=0. | Unrun |
| D3 — inspect | While A=1 and B=Z, aim at B's connector and press `I`; close with `Esc`. Aim at Y's connector and press `I`. Also inspect a component body. | B reports **Z**, its actual source OUT and AND B connections, and **no active driver**. Y reports **X** caused by uncertain B, without a false contention claim. Inspect must release the cursor, be readable/scrollable, and not allow world edits behind it. | Unrun |
| D4 — package and use | Place an SR separately. Aim at its cell for both region corners (`R`, then `T`), press Enter, review the draft, retain S/R/CLK input ports and Q output, and remove Q_bar as an exterior port. Validate, then **Publish module**. Select its hotbar item and place two copies apart. Place four independent Sources, each configured On value 1 and initial state Off, and connect one S and one R source to each copy. With Clock Link selected, right-click each instance's CLK port. Target each module body and press `I` to view its internal Q and Q_bar. Use the paused screen's **Step clock edge** control to apply the three rising-edge rows below. | Both copies begin Q=X. After rising edges: **A/B = 1/X, then 1/0, then 0/0**. A's changes do not change B's state. Internal Q_bar belongs to the selected instance and complements known Q. The published definition is available after reopening. | Unrun |
| D5 — save/reopen | After A/B reach 0/0, open `Esc` and choose **Save World**. Close and reopen the player; use **Browse saves/recovery** to open the saved world if needed. Inspect the AND, both instances, ports and connectors; check the paused screen. | Geometry, component positions, module design/version, the two distinct instances, connector topology, and saved clock frequency remain. The world clock starts stopped at level 0, ordinary simulation time is zero, all four S/R sources are Off and drive 0, and both Q outputs restart at **X/X**. Another rising edge with S=R=0 keeps X/X. | Unrun |

For D4, set the world clock to **10 Hz** on the paused screen. Each `Step clock edge` advances one transition; if the next transition is falling, step again before comparing the rising-edge row. For the offscreen check, after the second rising edge stop the clock, set A S=0/R=1 and B S=0/R=0, fly until both bodies are behind or outside the camera, start the clock with `P`, leave it running through at least the next rising edge, then return and inspect A/B=0/0. For D5, save *after* that third rising edge. A new ordinary save intentionally does not retain the live Q or clock-running state.
